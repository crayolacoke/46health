This Privacy Policy explains how 46 Health, LLC (“46 Health,” “we,” “us” or “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use fortysixhealth.com, any 46 Health patient portal or app, and the products and services offered through them (the “Service”). Capitalized terms not defined here, such as “Providers” and “Pharmacy,” have the meanings in our [Terms of Use](terms.html).

By using the Service, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you use the Service on someone else’s behalf, you confirm you are authorized to do so.

## 1. Who this covers

**Adults only.** The Service is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we will delete it unless the law requires us to keep it. If you believe a minor has given us information, email info@fortysixhealth.com.

**Your health information.** When you complete an intake, talk with a Provider or fill a prescription, the health information you share is used by your Providers (who practice through MD Integrations) and the Pharmacy (SureCare Specialty Pharmacy) to diagnose, treat and fill prescriptions. That information is protected under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and applicable state law, and how it is used and shared is described in the [Notice of Privacy Practices](notice-of-privacy-practices.html). Where 46 Health handles that information on behalf of your Providers or the Pharmacy, we do so as their business associate and only as HIPAA allows.

We call that health information “Protected Health Information.” This Privacy Policy covers all other personal information we handle, such as your name, contact details, account and order information, and how you use our website. Where this policy and HIPAA differ for Protected Health Information, HIPAA and the Notice of Privacy Practices control.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us.**

- Contact and account details: name, email, phone number, shipping and billing address, date of birth, sex, and your login credentials.
- Health information: your intake answers, medical history, medications, photos or documents you upload, and messages with your Providers. This is Protected Health Information, handled as described in Section 1.
- Identity verification: a photo ID or similar information when needed to confirm who you are before treatment.
- Order and payment information: the treatments you request, orders, prescriptions filled and payment details. Card numbers are entered into and stored by our payment processor, Stripe; 46 Health does not store full card numbers.
- Communications: emails, contact-form messages and support conversations with us.

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit our website, we and our service providers may collect your IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, links clicked, the site that referred you, and the time and date of your visit. We use cookies and similar technologies (such as your browser’s local storage) to keep the site working, for example to remember the treatments you add to your consultation list. We may use analytics tools to understand how the site is used. Your approximate location may be inferred from your IP address.

**Information from others.** We receive information from your Providers and the Pharmacy (for example, prescription and shipping status), from our payment processor (for example, whether a payment succeeded), and from service providers that help us verify identity or prevent fraud.

You can choose not to give us certain information, but some parts of the Service, including treatment, need it.

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- provide the Service: create your account, route your intake to a Provider, process orders and payments, coordinate with the Pharmacy and ship your medication;
- communicate with you about your account, orders, prescriptions and care, including on behalf of your Provider;
- provide customer support and respond to your requests;
- run, secure and improve the Service, prevent fraud, and verify identity;
- send you marketing about 46 Health, such as treatments you showed interest in, but only if you agreed to receive it, and never using Protected Health Information without your written authorization;
- comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Use.

We may create de-identified or aggregated information that can no longer reasonably identify you, and use it to understand and improve our services.

## 4. How we share information

**We do not sell your personal information.** We do not share your health information, or the treatment pages you view, with advertising companies.

We share personal information only as needed to run the Service:

- **Your Providers and MD Integrations,** so they can review your intake and care for you.
- **The Pharmacy (SureCare Specialty Pharmacy),** so it can prepare, dispense and ship your prescription.
- **Service providers** that work for us under contract and only for our purposes, such as Healthie (patient records, intake and messaging), Stripe (payments), shipping carriers, email and text-messaging providers, identity verification, hosting and customer support tools. Those that handle Protected Health Information sign business associate agreements.
- **Legal and safety reasons,** when we believe it is required by law or legal process, or necessary to protect the safety, rights or property of you, us or others.
- **Business transfers,** if 46 Health is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to this policy.
- **With your consent,** or at your direction.

Some parts of the Service link to third-party sites and services. Their own privacy policies apply to information you give them.

## 5. Your choices and rights

**Marketing.** You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email, and reply STOP to stop marketing texts. You will still receive messages needed for your account, orders and care.

**Cookies.** Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and local storage. If you do, some features, such as your saved consultation list, may not work. Where we use analytics or advertising cookies, we will honor Global Privacy Control signals as required by law. Our site does not respond to “Do Not Track” signals, because there is no common standard for them.

**Access, correction and deletion.** You can ask to see, correct, download or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@fortysixhealth.com from the email on your account. We will verify your identity before acting. Some information must be kept even after a deletion request, for example medical records that Providers and the Pharmacy are legally required to retain, payment records, and information needed to prevent fraud or meet legal obligations. Requests about your medical records are handled under the [Notice of Privacy Practices](notice-of-privacy-practices.html).

**State privacy rights.** Depending on where you live, state law may give you additional rights, such as the right to know what we collect, to correct or delete it, to opt out of the sale of personal information or targeted advertising, to limit the use of sensitive information, and to appeal if we decline your request. We do not sell personal information or use health information for targeted advertising. To make a request or an appeal, email info@fortysixhealth.com. You may use an authorized agent, and we will ask them for proof of authorization. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

## 6. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit and access controls, and we work with service providers that maintain their own safeguards. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password, keep it private, and tell us right away at info@fortysixhealth.com if you think your account has been compromised. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

## 7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as we need it to provide the Service, meet legal, tax, medical-record and accounting requirements, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. How long depends on the type of information and the law that applies to it. When we no longer need it, we delete or de-identify it.

## 8. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe through our patient platform. When you pay, your card details go directly to Stripe and are handled under Stripe’s privacy policy and security standards. We receive limited information, such as the last four digits of your card, the card type and whether the payment succeeded.

## 9. Where we operate

The Service is offered only in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States.
